Original simulated monthly operations by Mark Marosi · MIT material. Regions are invented business categories; values are not company accounts. The North and South revision removes West rows without changing retained values. Python measured the axes and outlined text. This prototype renders circles, line segments, bars and polygon regions with fixed page geometry, clips them to their plot areas and preserves row identity. Page zoom moves the whole drawing; it does not recalculate domains or ticks. Data and runtime are embedded for offline use.
