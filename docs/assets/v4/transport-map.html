Source observations


Original illustrative transport fixture · MIT. Coordinates describe an invented district, routes and stops, not real locations. Activity values are supplied simulated visits/day for overlapping asset populations; do not sum them. Map lengths and areas are not physical measurements. Python measured the axes and outlined text. This prototype renders circles, line segments, bars and polygon regions with fixed page geometry, clips them to their plot areas and preserves row identity. Page zoom moves the whole drawing; it does not recalculate domains or ticks. Data and runtime are embedded for offline use.
