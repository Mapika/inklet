## Use and download

Original SVG geometry by the Inklet project, under the repository MIT licence. No fonts or raster assets are required.

- [Primary SVG](assets/brand/inklet-logo.svg): wordmark for light backgrounds.
- [Dark-background SVG](assets/brand/inklet-logo-dark.svg): wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- [Monochrome SVG](assets/brand/inklet-logo-mono.svg): single-colour wordmark for white backgrounds.
- [Icon SVG](assets/brand/inklet-icon.svg): standalone symbol and favicon.
- [Wordmark SVG](assets/brand/inklet-wordmark.svg): lettering without the symbol.

The symbol combines a page silhouette, plot axes and one highlighted point.
The lettering uses custom paths with rounded terminals and consistent stroke
weight. Every version stays sharp at any scale.

Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the symbol height around the logo.
Use the icon alone at small sizes; use the wordmark at 110 px wide or larger.
